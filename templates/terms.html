1. Purpose of Application

This web application is an independent, client-side utility designed solely to help students of TKR Engineering College visualize and calculate attendance data.

2. Authentication & Data Handling

We do not store or save your login credentials (username or password). Authentication tokens are used in real time to fetch attendance summaries directly from the college portal and are discarded immediately after your session ends.

3. Disclaimer

This app is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by TKR Engineering College. Attendance records displayed here are for analytical and projection purposes only.
