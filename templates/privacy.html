Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Access

When you log in, your student roll number and credentials are strictly used to request and fetch attendance data directly from the official TKREC portal on your behalf.

2. No Credential Storage

We do not collect, save, or store your passwords, usernames, or attendance data in any server database or persistent storage.

3. Session In-Memory Processing

All authentication tokens and attendance details are held temporarily in memory during the request execution and discarded as soon as your page loads.

4. Analytics & Third-Party Sharing

No personal student information or login activity is ever shared, rented, or sold to third-party services or ad networks.
