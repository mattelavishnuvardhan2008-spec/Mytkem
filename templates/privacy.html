Privacy Policy

Your privacy is strictly respected. This policy outlines how your information is handled on this application.

1. Zero Credential Storage

When you log in, your student credentials (username and password) are transmitted securely and used exclusively to authenticate against the official college portal. We do not store, log, or save your credentials on any database or serverless logs.

2. Data Minimization

We only retrieve attendance records necessary to render your dashboard and calculate targets. No personal identifying information is persistently stored.
